# Loot & Craft

Mine ores, coal and gems, smelt ores into bars, craft items and sell them for Economy money. Items, drops and recipes can be changed per bot.

## Commands

- `/mine`: Goes mining and finds ores, coal and gems
- `/inventory`: Shows your materials and items
- `/recipes`: Lists all smelting and crafting recipes
- `/smelt`: Smelts ores into bars
- `/craft`: Crafts items from bars and materials
- `/sell`: Sells items for money
